Creator Autopilot

A personal Windows desktop app that helps one creator run their own YouTube channel: see how the channel is doing, research topics, produce Shorts with AI tools, and schedule uploads.

The app runs on the creator's own computer. It is not a web service, it has no accounts of its own, and it is not offered to the public. It uses the YouTube API Services (the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API) to read the creator's own channel data and, with permission, to upload and schedule videos to that same channel.

What it does

Dashboard

Subscribers, views, watch time, average view duration, audience retention, traffic sources and revenue for the creator's own channel.

Research

Searches public YouTube videos on a topic, ranks them by views per day, and tracks competitor channels to find video ideas.

Production

Turns an approved idea into a script, AI video clips, a voiceover, music, captions, a thumbnail, a title and a description, with the creator approving each step.

Scheduler

Puts finished videos on a posting calendar. It uploads each video to the creator's channel as private with a scheduled publish time, adds captions and playlists, and marks AI-generated content with YouTube's disclosure.

Your data

Channel data and sign-in tokens stay on the creator's computer, and the sign-in tokens are encrypted by Windows. Nothing is sent to the app's developer. Read the full privacy policy.
