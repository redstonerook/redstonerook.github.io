Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what AI YouTube Studio ("the app") accesses, how it uses and stores that information, and how you can remove it. The app is a personal Windows desktop program. It runs only on your computer, has no servers, and does not send your data to its developer.

The app uses YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API). By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (www.google.com/policies/privacy).

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, you choose which permissions to grant. The app asks only for what its features need:

PermissionWhat the app does with it
View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly)Reads your channel's name, picture, subscriber/view/video counts, your list of videos and their statistics, and your playlists, to show the dashboard and research pages.
View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly)Reads views, watch time, average view duration, audience retention and traffic sources for your channel and videos.
View monetary YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics-monetary.readonly)Reads estimated revenue, to show earnings on the dashboard.
Upload videos (youtube.upload)Only if you turn on direct upload: uploads the videos you scheduled to your own channel, as private with the publish time you chose.
Manage your YouTube account (youtube.force-ssl)Only if you turn on direct upload: adds a captions file and custom thumbnail to videos you uploaded with the app, adds them to the playlist you chose, and creates a playlist when you ask it to. The app never deletes videos and never edits anything you didn't ask it to.

The app also uses public YouTube data (search results, public video and channel statistics) for topic research and competitor tracking.

How the information is stored

  • Everything is stored only on your computer, in the app's data folder (%APPDATA%\AI YouTube Studio).
  • Your Google sign-in tokens and any AI service keys you enter are encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI), so only your Windows account can read them.
  • Channel statistics are refreshed from YouTube whenever you sync, and research results are kept so you can come back to them. You can delete saved searches, competitors, ideas and projects in the app at any time.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share your YouTube data, does not show ads, and does not send your data to its developer or any analytics service. Your YouTube data is used only to provide the features described above, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

When you use the AI features, the app sends only the text needed for that task to the AI service you set up, using your own API key:

  • Google Gemini API: topic names and the titles and statistics of public research videos (to explain why they work and suggest an angle), your idea and script text (to write scripts, titles and descriptions), and scene descriptions (to generate video clips).
  • ElevenLabs: the narration text of your script (to make the voiceover) and a short description of the music you want.
  • Kling (only if you choose it): scene descriptions, to generate video clips.

These services handle that text under their own privacy policies. The app does not send them your analytics, revenue, sign-in tokens or any other channel data.

Removing your data and revoking access

  • In the app: Google connection → Sign out removes the channel's sign-in tokens and deletes that channel's stored data, research, projects and schedule from your computer.
  • Revoke access at Google: in addition to the app's own deletion, you can revoke the app's access to your data at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After that, the app can no longer read or change anything on your channel.
  • Remove everything: uninstall the app, then delete the folder %APPDATA%\AI YouTube Studio.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is intended for use by the channel's adult owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
